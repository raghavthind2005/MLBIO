ATTENTION COMPUTATION NOTES
===========================

Gemma-4-31B-it (bfloat16, eager attention) runs a single teacher-forcing forward
pass over the full reconstructed conversation. A hook on every language-model
decoder self-attention layer captures weights [1, heads, q, kv], averages over
heads, extracts the output (assistant) token rows, and sums weights across each
token group of interest (visual, system, instruction) — giving the fraction of
each output position's attention budget allocated to that group. Values are then
averaged across all 60 decoder layers. The hook discards the full weight matrix
immediately to avoid OOM.

Visual tokens are contiguous runs of <image_soft_token> in the flat sequence
(~240–276 per image for this benchmark). Turn boundaries are identified via
<|turn> delimiters with role labels (system/user/model).

For rethink, the sequence is: [system][user: image+Q][assistant: think0+ans0]
[user: text-only re-examine][assistant: think1+ans1]. Output positions are split
at the model-turn boundary into turn-0 and turn-1 halves. attn_visual_output_turn0
and attn_visual_output_turn1 are the mean visual attention over each half —
quantifying how much each reasoning pass attended to the single shared image.

The decay curves interpolate each sample's per-position visual attention to 20
bins on a [0,1] relative axis, then average across samples. For rethink, the two
turns are interpolated separately.

Measurement note: forced t0 (0.097) slightly exceeds rethink t0 (0.086) despite
turn-0 being structurally identical in both. Forced lacks an explicit turn boundary,
so turn-0 positions are inferred as those with zero attention to the re-injected
image — small numerical non-zeros can misclassify a few positions, biasing the
estimate upward. Not a real behavioral difference.
